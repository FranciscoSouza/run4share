run4share privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

run4share turns your workouts into images you can share. This page explains what the app does with your information. In short: your data stays on your phone. run4share has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

What run4share stores, and where

Everything below is stored only on your phone, inside the app.

  • Activities. Activities you add by hand, and activities copied from Strava, Health Connect or Apple Health if you connect them: name, sport, date and time, distance, duration, pace or speed, heart rate, calories and elevation, and, for the charts, how heart rate, pace and altitude changed during the activity.
  • Your images (My Creations). The images you save or share, and the settings used to make them (which photo, how it was cropped, the sticker theme, position and colours), so you can edit them again.
  • Settings. Units, language, which format and theme you used last, and similar preferences.
  • Strava sign-in. If you connect Strava, the access tokens Strava gives run4share, kept in your phone's secure storage (Keychain on iPhone, encrypted storage on Android), plus your Strava name to label the connection.

run4share does not send any of this to its developers or anyone else.

Photos

You choose a photo in your phone's own photo picker (on Android and on iPhone): run4share gets only the photo you pick and has no access to the rest of your library, so it never asks for photo permission. It keeps a copy of that photo inside the app only so you can edit the image again later; the copy is deleted when you delete the images made with it, and is never uploaded. When you save an image, run4share adds it to your Photos, in a "run4share" album (on iPhone it asks once for permission to add photos).

Strava

Connecting Strava is optional. When you connect:

  • You sign in on Strava's own page and choose what to allow. run4share asks to read your profile (only your name is used) and your activities, including private ones, so you can make images of them. run4share can't post to or change your Strava account.
  • To finish signing in, and to renew access from time to time, the app sends a one-time code or a renewal token to a small run4share service hosted on Cloudflare. That service adds run4share's Strava app credentials, asks Strava for access tokens, and passes them straight back to your phone. It does not store or log them.
  • The app then reads your activities directly from Strava and keeps copies on your phone, as described above. If an activity has photos on Strava, the app shows small versions of them on the activity, and when you pick one it downloads it to your phone to use in your image. It never uploads anything to Strava.

When you disconnect Strava in Settings, run4share asks Strava to revoke its access and deletes every activity and photo it copied from Strava from your phone. Activities you added by hand stay.

Your use of Strava is covered by Strava's own privacy policy: https://www.strava.com/legal/privacy

Health Connect (Android)

Connecting Health Connect is optional. run4share asks to read your workouts and, for them, distance, calories, heart rate, route and steps (the steps are read alongside each workout by the library run4share uses; run4share doesn't use or keep them), and to read data older than 30 days. It never writes to Health Connect. The data is read on your phone and kept in run4share's own storage, like Strava activities; it is never sent anywhere. When you disconnect in Settings, run4share removes those workouts and gives the permissions back. You can also change the permissions any time in Health Connect.

run4share's use of data from Health Connect follows the Health Connect Permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Apple Health (iPhone)

Connecting Apple Health is optional. run4share asks to read your workouts and, for them, distance, calories, heart rate and route. It never writes to Apple Health. The data is read on your iPhone and kept in run4share's own storage; it is never sent anywhere, and never used for advertising. When you disconnect in Settings, run4share removes those workouts from its storage. To stop run4share reading Apple Health, use the Health app: Profile › Apps › run4share.

Purchases

run4share Pro is a one-time purchase made through Google Play or the App Store. They handle the payment; run4share never sees your payment details. The app only learns whether you own Pro, and remembers that on your phone.

Sharing

When you share an image, run4share hands it to the app you pick (Instagram, messages, email...). What happens to it next is up to you and that app. Copy puts a sticker image on your phone's clipboard so you can paste it; any app you paste into can read it, as with anything you copy.

Ratings, feedback and invites

  • Rate run4share opens the App Store or Google Play.
  • Send feedback opens a new issue on run4share's public GitHub page. Anything you write there is public.
  • Invite a friend shares a message with a link to the app through the app you pick. run4share doesn't learn who you invited.

Children

run4share is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age for digital services in your country).

Deleting your data

  • Delete an image: My Creations, open it, delete. Copies saved to Photos stay in Photos until you delete them there.
  • Delete an activity you added: its menu on the Activities tab.
  • Delete everything Strava-related: Settings, Strava, Disconnect.
  • Delete everything from Health Connect or Apple Health: Settings, the health source, Disconnect.
  • Delete everything: uninstall run4share. This removes all its data from your phone. Images you saved to Photos stay in Photos.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date, and the app will point to it.
