# Onboarding photos

Four portrait JPGs, Francisco's own photos (no stock or other apps'
images), named `page1.jpg` … `page4.jpg`:

- at least 1080 px wide, at most 400 KB each;
- faces and busy detail away from where stickers sit: the top 35 % and
  the bottom 30 % of the frame.

After adding one, list its file name in `onboardingPhotos` in
`lib/features/onboarding/onboarding_art.dart`. Pages without a photo
show a flat colour.
