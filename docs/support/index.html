We usually reply within two working days. Tell us your phone model and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Connecting Strava

  • On Home, tap Connect Strava, then Connect with Strava. Strava's own page opens: sign in and tap Authorize. You return to run4share and your latest activities appear.
  • run4share only reads your activities. It can't post to or change your Strava account.
  • If Strava says the limit of connected athletes has been exceeded (error 403): run4share is new and Strava currently lets only a limited number of athletes connect. We have asked Strava for more. Meanwhile you can use Health Connect or add an activity by hand (the + on Home).
  • Activities not showing? Pull down on Home to sync. Private activities are included.

Disconnecting Strava and your data

  • Settings › Strava › Disconnect. run4share asks Strava to revoke its access and deletes every activity and photo it copied from Strava from your phone.
  • You can also revoke access on Strava: Settings › My Apps › run4share › Revoke Access.
  • run4share keeps your data only on your phone. There is no account and nothing to delete on our side. See the privacy policy.

Health Connect (Android)

  • Settings › Health Connect › Connect, and allow all the permissions.
  • No workouts? The app that recorded them must share with Health Connect too: Android Settings › Health Connect › App permissions › that app › allow.

run4share Pro

  • Pro is a one-time purchase, no subscription. It unlocks charts, route styles, Pro themes, templates and stickers, photo effects, and removes the watermark.
  • New phone or reinstalled the app? Settings › Restore purchase, signed in with the same Google account. You won't be charged again.
  • Refunds are handled by Google Play.

Photos

  • run4share uses your phone's own photo picker, so it never needs access to your whole library, and photos are never uploaded.
