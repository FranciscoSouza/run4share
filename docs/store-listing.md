# Store listing drafts

Drafts for Google Play and the App Store, 16 Nov 2026 (phase 18). Have
native speakers review pt-BR, es, fr and de, as with the app's strings.

Rules followed:
- **Strava:** only "connect Strava" / "with Strava". No wording that
  suggests Strava made or endorses run4share (Strava's brand guidelines).
  Screenshots that show Strava data keep the "Powered by Strava" mark.
- **Health source:** Play text says Health Connect, App Store text says
  Apple Health. Swap the one bracketed phrase.
- **Pro:** described as one payment, as the Pro screen says. Don't name
  a price in the text; the stores show it.
- **Limits:** title up to 30 characters, Play short description up to 80,
  full description up to 4,000. App Store subtitle up to 30.

Title (all languages): **run4share**
